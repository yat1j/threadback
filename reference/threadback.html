Why Threadback

You come back to a group chat with a thousand unread messages. Threadback reads them on your device, finds what needs your attention, tracks deadlines and changed plans, and shows you the exact messages behind every answer. No account. No upload. No guesswork you can't verify.

Features

01 Catch-up Briefing

Choose 'since I last read' or any date range and get what needs your attention first, then deadlines, decisions and a clear summary.

Try it
02 Personal Priorities

Mentions of you, requests aimed at you and commitments you made, with explicit tasks clearly separated from inferred ones.

Try it
03 Decisions and Changes

See the earlier plan next to the latest supported plan, with timestamps, sources and a flag where the chat is unclear.

Try it
04 Ask Your Chat

Ask in plain language. Answers come only from your chat, cite the exact messages, and say so when there isn't enough evidence.

Private by design

Stays on your device

Your chat, your questions and your summaries. None of it is uploaded.

What we download

Only the AI model files, cached by your browser. They never contain your chat.

Honest limits

Needs desktop Chrome or Edge with WebGPU. The first run downloads the model. AI can make mistakes, so check the linked source messages.

Verify it yourself: open your browser's network tab.

Limitations

Works best on desktop Chrome or Edge. Summaries may miss or misread things, so always check sources. Threadback is not affiliated with WhatsApp.
